# Specter

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1364/364/2025-10-21_02-29-57-png.png)

---

"Specters," the mod's equivalent of Ghosts from Destiny, can be crafted with Specter Cores (found in Jungle Pyramids and Desert Temples), Copper Ingots, and a dye color of your choice. (Use a mod such as JEI to view recipes in-game).

Specters prevent the player from dying (under most circumstances), and are quick to heal the player if their health is too far below full.

Updates are planned! Please leave a comment with any suggestions!

(This mod was created for CurseForge's "Forge the Future: Minecraft ModJam 2025")
